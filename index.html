StormInvoice

AI invoice automation for accountants

Chartered accountants and financial services firms told us the same thing in discovery interviews: invoice entry was manual, slow and error-prone. I turned their pain points into a prioritized MVP spec, then took the product from a GPT-4 prototype to a self-hosted, fine-tuned Llama-3 model with OCR.

About 75% lower cost per invoice and 5x the throughput of manual entry.

Clinical decision support
